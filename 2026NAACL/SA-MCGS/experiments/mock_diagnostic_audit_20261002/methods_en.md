# Replacement diagnostic methods text — pending manuscript integration

We reanalyzed 90 archived synthetic mock runs comprising two search implementations, three topology families (DAGs, simple cycles with acyclic prefix/suffix nodes, and compound cycles linked by bridges), three scales, and five repetitions. Each run used 100 iteration slots. The TreeMCTS implementation maintained path-indexed tree states and evaluated the unique nodes encountered by a random walk of at most 30 transitions. A simplified SCC-window proxy used SCC-biased seed selection, local windows of three to five nodes, per-node score accumulation, and a window transposition cache. The evaluator generated noisy scores from synthetic node-risk values; no LLM was called. The proxy is a diagnostic implementation and does not instantiate the full SA-MCGS evidence-memory and core-selection pipeline. Methods shared topology families and iteration counts, but used different random seeds and were not matched by realized graph or evaluator cost.

We report each method–topology cohort separately (15 runs per row). The tree-node ratio is the final number of allocated tree nodes divided by the number of physical graph nodes, averaged across runs. Depth-cap reaching is the fraction of random-walk paths containing 31 nodes, corresponding to 30 transitions. The archive does not retain the final terminal status needed to recover exact terminal-conditioned truncation. Tree metrics are inapplicable to the window proxy. The saved node-Q range is the maximum minus minimum strictly positive final Q over physical graph nodes; it is not a root-action range or standard deviation. Synthetic anomaly Hit@3 indicates whether the generator's anomalous node appears among the three highest-ranked nodes. Complete final node-Q vectors were not archived, so Q ranges and ranks are reaggregated from saved run summaries; Hit@3 is checked against the saved ranks.

These runs illustrate path-state duplication and cap-reaching in this implementation. They do not isolate a universal effect of cyclic topology: TreeMCTS also has low anomaly Hit@3 on DAGs, and its path-backpropagated window means differ from the proxy's node-specific score estimates. Consequently, this diagnostic does not establish production SA-MCGS's performance at matched compute or a general failure of MCTS convergence.
