\begin{revadded}
\paragraph{SA-referenced budget comparison.}

We compare enhanced repeated-sampling LEA and Full-SCC with fixed historical \samcgs{} outputs. Across five backbones and 80 variants per backbone, recorded or calibrated \samcgs{} reference budgets total approximately 89.2 million input and output tokens (Appendix~\ref{app:equal_budget}). Both baselines receive the same budget for each backbone and case.

\samcgs{} achieves higher \riskall{} than repeated-sampling LEA on all five backbones (Figure~\ref{fig:equal_budget_tradeoff}). The gains are 33.75 percentage points on GPT-4o and 25.00 points on Qwen2.5-72B; \samcgs{} reaches 75.00\% on both. Kimi illustrates a different coverage--compactness balance: Full-SCC reaches higher \riskall{} (52.50\% versus 32.50\%) but retains 86.40\% of the graph on average, compared with 48.15\% for \samcgs{}.


\end{revadded}
