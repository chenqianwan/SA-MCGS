\paragraph{\revdel{Motivating search diagnostic.}}

\revdel{We compare TreeMCTS on acyclic and strongly connected document graphs with \samcgs{} on the same \scc{} setting. The comparison uses matched components, the same local risk task, and equal rollout budgets. Table~1 reports state expansion, rollout truncation, Q-spread, and Hit@3, as defined below.}

\revdel{Table~1 uses four ablation diagnostics.  \textbf{Expansion}
is the number of search states expanded relative to the physical
component size.  \textbf{Truncation} is the fraction of rollouts stopped
by the depth limit before a terminal state; loops can prevent termination (Moerland et~al., 2020).  \textbf{Q-spread} is the range of estimated
action values at the root, a proxy for whether search can separate
promising actions under UCT-style selection (Kocsis and Szepesv{\'a}ri, 2006).
\textbf{Hit@3} reports whether a repair-relevant endpoint appears in the
top three ranked records.}
